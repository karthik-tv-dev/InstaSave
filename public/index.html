GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

A few useful details before you get started.

How do I use InstaSave?

Copy a supported Instagram post or Reel URL, paste it into the field at the top, and select Get Media. If media is returned, choose one of the available download options.

Why is no media showing for my link?

The post may be private, restricted, unavailable, or unsupported. Instagram may also change how links can be accessed. Check that the URL is correct and try again.

Can I download private Instagram content?

InstaSave is not intended to bypass privacy controls or access restrictions. Use only content you own or are authorized to save.

Where are the downloaded files saved?

This depends on your browser and device. Most browsers save downloaded files to their default Downloads folder unless you choose another location.

Does every Instagram link work?

No. Supported formats and availability depend on the Instagram link and the connected media API. Some links may not return downloadable files.

Can I save someone else's content?

Only save content when you have permission or another lawful basis to do so. Respect creators' rights, privacy, and the applicable platform terms.
